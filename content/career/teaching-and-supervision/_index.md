Between 2017 and 2025 I [supervised 22 student](/career/teaching-and-supervision/supervision-of-students/) projects at TU Ilmenau: nine master's theses, six project seminars, two seminar papers, and five student research assistants. Most of them dealt with stereo vision, calibration, or 3D reconstruction.

Outside the university I mentor schoolgirls in STEM through [CyberMentor](/career/teaching-and-supervision/cybermentor/), and at [UNIKAT](/career/teaching-and-supervision/unikat/), the makerspace at TU Ilmenau, I run introductory courses and workshops.
