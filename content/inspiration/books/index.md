## Story

I love traveling and always make time for bookstores in every new place. Even if I don’t understand the language, I’m drawn to the art, the illustrations, the way books are made. There’s something special about flipping through pages full of stories I can’t read but still feel. That’s where I find my inspiration — quiet, visual, and full of wonder. 🌍📖✨
