## Motivation

I once wanted to learn the art of violin making. However, I never actually submitted my application.

Then I met Alexandra and Pascal, two professional violin makers. Since I don’t currently need another violin, I thought: why not build a ukulele professionally instead? The process doesn’t take quite as long, and it’s a great way to learn the craft and get an insight into the world of instrument making.

But I didn’t want to do everything the traditional way. That’s why I’m also using, among other things, a CO₂ laser cutter. I’ll show you exactly where and how it comes into play throughout the build.
