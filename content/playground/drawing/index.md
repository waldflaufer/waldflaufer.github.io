## Where it started

Drawing is the thing I do when I want to switch off. I started by copying the work of professional character designers, above all [Chris Ayers](https://www.chrisayersdesign.com/) and [Terryl Whitlatch](https://www.artstation.com/terrylwhitlatch/), to learn how they build a character.

After that I wanted a way to come up with characters of my own. I tried a few things, showed the sketches to friends, and asked whether they wanted to join. Six of us ended up drawing together. I call the method _Random Fusion Character Design_.

## Random Fusion Character Design

<figure>
  <a href="rfcd-concept.png">
    <img src="rfcd-concept.png" alt="Random Fusion Character Design: how it works" style="height:auto;">
  </a>
  <figcaption>How it works, with four of my results.</figcaption>
</figure>

**How it works:**

1. A small Python script draws **random profession + creature combinations**. In the beginning we used slips of paper.
2. The group votes for the most inspiring one.
3. Everyone has a week to draw their own version of that character.
4. The results are shown, compared, and discussed. Then the next round.

We were six. Later the rounds got further apart, because a week is short when life is full. But it was a lot of fun, and the characters are still here.

Sometimes we research together what a creature should look like. Nobody remembers offhand what a monkey with a skull-like face looks like, so we collect references from wherever they turn up.
