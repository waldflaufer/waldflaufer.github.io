## Story

I started figure skating as an adult, at 30, with [EC Ilmenau](https://eci-eiskunstlauf.de/website/),
but I have liked being on ice for as long as I can remember.

What I learn on the ice goes into my [Skate Atlas]({{< relref "/skate-atlas" >}}) -
technique, mistakes, rhythm and patterns. That part is written in German.
