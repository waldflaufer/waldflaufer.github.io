I teach cameras to see in three dimensions.

More precisely: I build stereo vision systems that calibrate themselves, and I
work on the question of how good 3D data has to be before a robot and a person
can safely share a workspace.

In August 2026 I defended my PhD at TU Ilmenau on exactly that —
*Stereo Vision Systems with Autonomous Calibration and High Data Quality for
Integration into Human–Robot Collaboration Systems.*

![After the defence at TU Ilmenau, August 2026 — with my colleagues and friends Martin and Yan, who built my doctoral hat.](defence.jpg)

Three project lines run through all of it: **FPGA-based stereo vision**,
**autonomous robot calibration**, and **detecting transparent objects with AI** —
the last one being harder than it sounds, because a transparent object mostly
shows you what is behind it.
