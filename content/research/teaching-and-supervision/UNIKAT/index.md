{{< meta >}}UNIKAT - the workshop for students, TU Ilmenau, part of the official FabLab · Executive Committee Member & Teaching Assignment (voluntary and unpaid) · since February 2017{{< /meta >}}

A student-run makerspace with a focus on woodworking, laser cutting,
embroidery and sewing. I support the workshop's expansion, manage new
equipment, and design hands-on workshops - woodworking projects, soldering
workshops, sewing workshops, and introductory courses for embroidery, sewing
and overlock machines.

I was also involved in setting up the TextilLab, documenting processes in the
wiki, and sharing what I know with the student community.
