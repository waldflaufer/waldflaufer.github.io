Between 2017 and 2025 I supervised master's theses, project seminars, seminar
papers and student assistants at TU Ilmenau, in the Group for Quality Assurance
and Industrial Image Processing. Most of the topics circle around the same
questions as my own work: stereo vision, calibration, 3D reconstruction and
what happens when a surface refuses to cooperate.

Titles are given in the language they were written in.
